Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

At Sentaient ("we", "our", or "us"), we believe privacy is a fundamental human right. This Privacy Policy details how we handle and protect your personal information when you use the Sentaient mobile and web applications.

2. Data We Collect & How It Is Used

  • Microphone Audio: Used solely in real-time to transcribe your voice conversations and execute hands-free voice commands with your 3D avatar companion. Audio streams are processed securely in real-time and are never sold or used for cross-app advertising.
  • Camera & Vision Data: Used strictly for augmented reality (AR) placement and optional real-time emotion/expression detection on your device. Camera frames are processed on-device and are not recorded or uploaded to remote databases.
  • Conversation Text: Stored locally on your device or in your secure private database so you can view conversation history and action plans. You can delete individual chats or purge all history at any time.
  • Biometric Data (Face ID / Fingerprint): If enabled, authentication occurs entirely within your device's native hardware Secure Enclave. Sentaient never accesses, collects, or transmits your biometric data.

3. Third-Party AI Subprocessors

To generate natural language responses and lifelike voice speech, text queries are processed through enterprise API endpoints (e.g., Google Cloud AI, LiveKit, ElevenLabs). These subprocessors are bound by strict enterprise data agreements prohibiting the use of customer data for training public foundation models.

4. Zero Cross-App Tracking & No Data Selling

Sentaient does not sell your personal information, does not track you across third-party websites or apps, and does not serve targeted third-party advertising.

5. Right to Delete (Account & Data Erasure)

You have complete control over your data. You may delete your account and all associated messages and data at any time directly in the app under Settings > Delete Account. Upon confirmation, your user records and stored messages are permanently eradicated.

6. Security

We employ industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest to safeguard all account credentials and private conversation stores.

7. Contact

For data inquiries or privacy assistance, please contact our Data Protection team at: privacy@sentaient.com.
