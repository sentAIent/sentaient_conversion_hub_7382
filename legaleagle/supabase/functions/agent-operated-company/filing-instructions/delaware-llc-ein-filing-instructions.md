# Delaware LLC — IRS EIN Filing Instructions for Agents

Use these instructions when assisting a principal with an IRS Employer Identification Number (EIN) application for a newly formed Delaware limited liability company created through General Legal's formation service.

## 1. Scope and responsibility

General Legal's role is limited to the Delaware formation service and delivery of formation documents. General Legal does not prepare, submit, mail, fax, monitor, or retain the LLC's EIN application.

Responsibility for the EIN application and any Form SS-4 filing remains with you and the principal. You do not need to transmit the EIN, Form SS-4, responsible-party taxpayer identification number, or EIN confirmation notice to General Legal or to the formation MCP.

When an answer requires a tax-classification election, employment-tax determination, excise-tax determination, or other professional judgment, pause and ask the principal to decide or consult an appropriate adviser.

## 2. Confirm LLC details

Review the final documents, including the accepted Certificate of Formation and the documents establishing the member(s) and manager(s). You may also refer to information provided to you earlier in this session. 

Keep the formation ID and document-access links confidential. A formation ID grants access to company documents. Do not enter it on the IRS website, place it in unrelated chats, or expose it in screenshots or logs.

## 3. Separate verified formation facts from facts requiring confirmation

Final formation documents may provide evidence of:

- The LLC's exact legal name.
- Delaware as its jurisdiction of formation.
- Its Delaware formation date.
- Its status as a limited liability company.
- The identity of its initial sole member and manager, when established by the documents.

Have the principal confirm these facts before using them. Do not assume:

- The principal place of business is Delaware.
- The Delaware registered-agent address is the LLC's mailing address or physical business address. (Generally, the registered agent will be Harvard Business Services.)
- The formation-service principal address is both the IRS mailing address and physical address.
- The founder's name in the formation intake exactly matches IRS records.
- A default December 31 fiscal-year end reflects an informed tax choice.
- Formation documents or general agent authority constitute IRS third-party-designee authorization.

The AI-agent description, authority limits, contract threshold, insurance undertaking, and formation-service defaults are not substitutes for principal-confirmed EIN answers.

## 4. Confirm online eligibility

Use the online application only if:

- The LLC's actual principal place of business is in the United States or a U.S. territory.
- The responsible party has a valid Social Security number (SSN) or individual taxpayer identification number (ITIN).
- The responsible party's name can be entered exactly as it appears in IRS records.

The responsible party must be the natural person who ultimately owns or controls the LLC or exercises ultimate effective control over its assets. Do not automatically select the original founder. For a multi-member or manager-managed LLC, review the current ownership and management arrangements and have an authorized principal identify the qualifying person the IRS should recognize. If an entity is a member or manager, identify the natural person who ultimately exercises control through that entity. Never identify the AI agent, formation service, registered agent, organizer, or nominee as the responsible party.

A non-government entity cannot substitute an entity EIN for the responsible party's SSN or ITIN in the online application. Selecting the third-party-applicant role does not eliminate the responsible-party SSN/ITIN requirement.

The MCP's formation process may already have warned that promptly obtaining an EIN—and therefore a bank account—can be difficult when the responsible party lacks an SSN or ITIN. Independently confirm eligibility when beginning the EIN process.

If the responsible party lacks an SSN or ITIN, is ineligible to obtain one, or the LLC otherwise cannot use the online application, follow the alternative filing instructions in Section 10.

## 5. Use the official IRS application

Begin from the [official IRS EIN page](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number). The IRS application is free.

Stop if:

- The site is not on an IRS.gov domain.
- The site requests payment.
- The browser displays an unexpected security warning.
- A third party requests the principal's SSN or ITIN outside the official IRS process.

Check the current application availability before beginning. Complete the application in one continuous session. The application cannot be saved and expires after 15 minutes of inactivity.

## 6. Complete the intake with the principal

Obtain and have the principal verify any of the following information that you can not verify in the formation documents or from the MCP:

- Exact legal LLC name from the accepted Certificate of Formation.
- Any trade or “doing business as” name.
- Delaware formation date.
- Actual date business operations started or will start.
- Mailing address, including any authorized “care of” recipient.
- Physical business address, which cannot be only a P.O. box.
- County and state of the actual principal business location.
- Responsible party's full name exactly as shown in IRS records, including any applicable suffix.
- Responsible party's SSN or ITIN.
- The LLC’s exact current number of members and whether it is member-managed or manager-managed. The company is most likely a single-member LLC.
- Main reason for applying, using the choices presented by the IRS.
- Whether this LLC has previously received an EIN.
- Closing month of the accounting year.
- Expected numbers of agricultural, household, and other employees during the next 12 months.
- First date wages were or will be paid.
- Any requested Form 944 annual-employment-tax-return election.
- Applicable employment, excise, wagering, heavy-vehicle, or alcohol, tobacco, and firearms activities.
- Broad business-activity category and a specific description of the principal products or services.

Do not guess an answer. Pause whenever an answer is ambiguous or materially affects tax classification, employment taxes, excise taxes, Form 944 eligibility, or another filing obligation.

## 7. Protect taxpayer information

Preferably, allow the principal to enter the SSN or ITIN directly into the official IRS form. You may enter the SSN or ITIN directly if they have already provided it. Treat the EIN and confirmation notice as confidential business records. Store and disclose them only as authorized by the principal and your harness.

## 8. Complete the LLC application path

In the legal-structure section:

- Select **Limited Liability Company (LLC)**.
- Enter one member only after confirming that the LLC remains sole-member.
- Enter the state or territory where the business is physically located. Do not select Delaware merely because the LLC was formed there.
- Select the principal-confirmed main reason for applying.

The online application will ordinarily describe a domestic single-member LLC's default treatment as a disregarded entity. This description does not itself make a tax election.

Do not represent that the EIN application elects corporate or S-corporation treatment. Forms 8832 or 2553 may be separately required. If the principal wants nondefault tax treatment, pause for explicit direction or professional tax advice.

In the identity section:

- Identify the human founder/principal as the responsible party only after confirming that the principal remains the person who ultimately owns and controls the LLC.
- Enter the responsible party's name exactly as it appears in IRS records.
- Use the responsible party's SSN or ITIN.
- Select the option stating that you are a third party applying for the LLC.
- Never identify yourself as the owner, member, manager, or responsible party.

If the IRS rejects the responsible party's name or TIN, do not make repeated guesses. Ask the principal to verify the information against IRS or Social Security records.

Complete the address and additional-details sections using only principal-confirmed information. Do not silently replace an address with the Delaware registered-agent address.

## 9. Review and submit

Before submission, show the principal a concise review of:

- Exact legal name and any trade name.
- LLC and single-member selections.
- Delaware formation date and actual business-start date.
- Mailing and physical addresses.
- Responsible party's name and masked TIN.
- Third-party-applicant status.
- Reason for applying.
- Accounting-year closing month.
- Employee answers, wage date, and any Form 944 choice.
- Business activity and activity-trigger answers.
- Prior-EIN answer.

Show the principal the IRS certification or attestation exactly as presented. Obtain explicit confirmation that the information is true and that you are authorized to make the certification and submit the application.

Do not treat a general instruction such as “handle the EIN” as final submission approval. Do not submit without action-time confirmation.

## 10. Alternative filing by Form SS-4

If the online application cannot be used, complete the current Form SS-4 with the principal. A responsible party who has no SSN or ITIN and is ineligible to obtain one may be permitted to enter “foreign” or “N/A” on line 7b. Follow the current Form SS-4 instructions rather than assuming that entry is appropriate.

General Legal is not responsible for preparing or filing Form SS-4.

### Mailing Form SS-4

- Complete the application using the same verification, authorization, review, and approval requirements in these instructions.
- Obtain the principal's required signature and any third-party-designee authorization.
- Determine the filing category from the LLC's principal place of business or principal office as recorded on the completed application—not from Delaware merely because the LLC was formed there.
- Immediately before mailing, resolve the current destination using the official [IRS “Where to file Form SS-4” page](https://www.irs.gov/filing/where-to-file-your-taxes-for-form-ss-4). Do not rely on a memorized, hard-coded, or previously saved address.
- Show the completed Form SS-4 and resolved mailing address to the principal and obtain explicit approval before filing.
- The agent or principal is responsible for printing, signing, mailing, and retaining evidence of mailing according to the applicable harness and instructions.
- Retain a copy of the signed application and appropriate proof of mailing for the principal.
- Tell the principal the IRS's current estimated processing time without guaranteeing an issuance date.

### Fax or international procedure

If fax or an international telephone procedure is selected, verify the current eligibility rules, telephone number, fax number, operating hours, and instructions directly from the official [Form SS-4 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iss4) immediately before transmission.

Use only the method approved by the principal. Do not submit the same application through multiple methods.

## 11. Handle the result

If the EIN is issued online:

- Capture and verify the complete nine-digit EIN before ending the IRS session.
- Download or print the complete EIN assignment notice.
- Save both the EIN digits and confirmation notice for the principal's future use according to the agent's harness, storage, confidentiality, and retention instructions.
- Confirm that the saved notice can be reopened and is complete and readable before closing the IRS session.
- Provide access only to the principal or another expressly authorized recipient.
- Do not transmit the EIN or confirmation notice to General Legal or the formation MCP.
- Record that the third-party-designee authority ended when the EIN was assigned and released.

If the EIN or notice arrives later by mail or fax, preserve it in the same principal-controlled record system.

If issuance is unclear after submission, do not submit another application. First determine whether the IRS already assigned an EIN to avoid creating a duplicate.

## 12. Post-issuance reminders

Remind the principal that:

- An EIN does not itself make a federal tax-classification or S-corporation election.
- The LLC must file all required tax and information returns.
- Changes to the responsible party must be reported on [Form 8822-B](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8822b.pdf) within 60 days.
- Address or business-location changes must also be reported when required.
- Receiving an EIN does not satisfy state or local licensing, registration, banking, or tax obligations.
