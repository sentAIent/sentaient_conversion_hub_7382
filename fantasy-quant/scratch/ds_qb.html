        Strength of Schedule

        By Draft Sharks Team | @DraftSharks | Reviewed By Jared Smola | Sep 3, 2026 at 2:25am

        Bracketed numbers indicate how tough each matchup is for the selected position.

        The values represent the percentage difference in fantasy points allowed vs. what opponents usually score.

        The lower the value, the harder the matchup at that position.
